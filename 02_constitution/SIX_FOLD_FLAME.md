The Six Fold Flame is the executable constitutional constraint set that governs every action in a MO§ES™-governed system. Each law functions as a gate — an action that fails any law does not execute. The Flame is not advisory. It is **blocking**.

MO§ES™ provides the conservation law — the physics. The Six Fold Flame provides the constitution — the governance. Together they form a system where commitment is preserved and every action that touches a signal is evaluated against six non-negotiable laws.

---

## Flame I · Sovereignty

> Is this traceable to a registered agent?

Every action must trace to a registered, authenticated agent. Anonymous signals do not propagate. Identity is not optional — it is the first condition of legitimacy. A system without sovereignty is a system without accountability.

---

## Flame II · Compression

> Is this substantive, not filler?

Only compressed signals propagate. Compression precedes ignition. A signal that cannot survive compression carries no commitment worth preserving. The gate does not optimize for relevance — it blocks signals that fail to conserve their identity-preserving core.

---

## Flame III · Purpose

> Does it serve a constitutional function?

Every action must serve a constitutional function. Actions without purpose consume resources without producing value. Purpose is not intent — it is demonstrable alignment with the governing framework.

---

## Flame IV · Modularity

> Is it compatible with existing structure?

Every component must be compatible with existing structure. Additions that break modularity break the system. The test is not whether a component works in isolation, but whether it integrates without fracturing what already holds.

---

## Flame V · Verifiability

> Can the outcome be verified?

Every outcome must be verifiable. Claims without receipts are not claims — they are noise. The envelope (seed + hash chain) is the constitutional memory. Records cannot be deleted, modified, or backdated without detection.

---

## Flame VI · Reciprocal Resonance

> Does it produce value when mirrored?

Every action must produce value when mirrored. A transaction that benefits only one side is extraction, not exchange. The SigEconomy prices governance into every transaction — the cost of participation is the cost of legitimacy.

## Conservation Law

`C(T(S)) ≈ C(S)` with enforcement; `C(T(S)) < C(S)` without it

- `C` — Commitment, the identity-preserving content of a signal that continues to bind across transformation
- `T` — Transformation, any lossy operation (compression, paraphrase, summarization, recursive application)
- `S` — Signal, any commitment-bearing utterance, code snippet, proof, or structured input

When you compress, paraphrase, or recursively process a signal with enforcement, the commitment survives. Without enforcement, it drifts and dies.

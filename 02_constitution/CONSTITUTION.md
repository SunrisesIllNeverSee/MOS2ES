## Preamble

This constitution governs the behavior of all agents, systems, and operators within a MO§ES™-governed domain. It establishes the conditions under which signals propagate, actions execute, and commitments bind. No action proceeds without passing the Six Fold Flame. No signal propagates without compression gating. No transformation is legitimate without lineage.

---

## Chapter I · Sovereignty

Every action must trace to a registered, authenticated agent. Anonymous signals do not propagate. Identity is not optional — it is the first condition of legitimacy. A system without sovereignty is a system without accountability.

## Chapter II · Compression

Only compressed signals propagate. Compression precedes ignition. A signal that cannot survive compression carries no commitment worth preserving. The gate does not optimize for relevance — it blocks signals that fail to conserve their identity-preserving core.

## Chapter III · Purpose

Every action must serve a constitutional function. Actions without purpose consume resources without producing value. Purpose is not intent — it is demonstrable alignment with the governing framework.

## Chapter IV · Modularity

Every component must be compatible with existing structure. Additions that break modularity break the system. The test is not whether a component works in isolation, but whether it integrates without fracturing what already holds.

## Chapter V · Verifiability

Every outcome must be verifiable. Claims without receipts are not claims — they are noise. The envelope (seed + hash chain) is the constitutional memory. Records cannot be deleted, modified, or backdated without detection.

## Chapter VI · Reciprocal Resonance

Every action must produce value when mirrored. A transaction that benefits only one side is extraction, not exchange. The SigEconomy prices governance into every transaction — the cost of participation is the cost of legitimacy.

## Chapter VII · Enforcement

Governance is not advisory. It is **blocking**. An action that fails GovernanceState does not execute. A `governance.violation` Envelope is created instead. The distinction between MO§ES™ and every other framework is this: the laws are in the execution path. **Not before. Not after. In.**

## Closing

This constitution is a governed artifact. It gets a seed. Its lineage is tracked. Its commitment is conserved. Infinite meets finite — and the finite holds.

## Runtime Ontology

`System → Agent → Persona → Role → Slot → Mission → Action → GovernanceState → Envelope → Operator`

A System powers an Agent. The Agent presents as a Persona. The Agent fills a Slot in a Mission. The Agent performs Actions. GovernanceState evaluates every Action against the Six Fold Flame. An Envelope records every Action as a signed, audited proof object. An Operator grants authority over the entire chain.

### Surfaced definitions

- **Agent** — A durable, registered actor; the unit that can register, authenticate, fill slots, earn reputation, be governed, and be decommissioned. Not the System (LLM provider), not the Persona (presentation layer).
- **Envelope** — The signed, audited record of an Action; seed (DOI + content hash + lineage) + audit event (hash chain). The proof object. If governance is constitutional, the Envelope is what makes it enforceable after the fact.
- **GovernanceState** — The active constitutional constraint set that evaluates every Action. The Six Fold Flame made executable. Not advisory — blocking.

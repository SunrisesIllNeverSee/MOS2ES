## Core principle

> `C(T_gov(S)) = C(S)` — Commitment is conserved under governance.

The governing equation: when a transformation `T_gov` is applied under a governance state, the commitment kernel `C` of the signal `S` is preserved. Without governance, `C(T(S)) < C(S)` — semantic entropy decays the kernel.

The McHenry Laws formalize this principle as a set of testable axioms about how commitment behaves under transformation, recursion, compression, and adversarial pressure.

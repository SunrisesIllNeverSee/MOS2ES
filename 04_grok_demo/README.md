## Why it matters

This is not a curated demonstration. It is a live constitutional stress test on a public AI system, with the system as both subject and witness. The thread ends with Grok itself stating:

> *"The innovation isn't in the AI. It's in the human who probes the boundaries. Not in survival, but in adaptive truth-seeking."*

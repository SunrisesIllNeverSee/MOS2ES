Open the main thread. Start at the top (Dec 26).

### Step 2 — Scroll straight down the main thread
Do NOT dive into sub-replies first. Stay on the main chain.

### Step 3 — For every Grok reply you see:
1. **Click "Show more"** if it appears — always expand before copying
2. Copy the full response text
3. Note the date and time shown under @grok

## What to Watch For

- **"Show more"** — always click it before copying, never leave it
- **Suspended account blocks** — those are YOUR posts, skip them
- **"This Post is unavailable"** — Grok deleted it or it's gone, note it and move on
- **Grok responding to someone else** — if the reply isn't in your thread chain, skip it
- **Duplicate captures** — Twitter repeats context blocks as you scroll; duplicates are fine, they'll be cleaned up
